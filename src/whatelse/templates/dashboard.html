Fresh ideas for any challenge, from five AI agents that think differently.

What Else? is for anyone stuck on a challenge and tired of the obvious answers: a small business, a career move, a project, an event. Describe it, and five creative agents (Reverse Thinking, Random Associations, Analogies, SCAMPER and Challenging Assumptions) build on each other's ideas. You save, discard or rework them, try the best ones, and the agents learn from what you liked and what worked.

  1. Open the sample challenge: a bookshop that wants more weekday visitors.
  2. Click Generate, then Save, Discard or Rework a few ideas.
  3. Look at the Idea Library and Experiments, then close the challenge as Achieved to see its summary.
